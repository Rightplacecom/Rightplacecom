📈 NIFTY 50 F&O Market Intelligence Product

Product Area: Fintech • Investment • Financial Analytics

A financial-market product designed to help users interpret multiple market indicators and convert complex market data into simpler signals.

Product Work
Identified the problem of interpreting multiple market indicators before making sense of short-term market conditions.
Analyzed 50+ market features and indicators across price, volume, Call OI, Put OI, and OI changes.
Prioritized 5 core market indicators for the primary product experience.
Designed 2 primary user-facing signals to simplify market interpretation.
Defined product metrics around signal accuracy, signal frequency, false-signal rate, engagement, and interpretation time.
Iterated on the product based on data quality, signal behavior, and clarity of user-facing outputs.

💳 Fintech Transaction Failure & Payment Experience

Product Area: Fintech • Payments • Consumer Experience

A product case study focused on improving the experience users face when payments fail, remain pending, or require additional action.

Product Work
Mapped the payment journey across 6 transaction states: initiated, processing, successful, failed, pending, and resolved.
Identified 5 major failure categories including insufficient balance, bank-side failures, timeout, authentication, and technical errors.
Designed requirements for clearer failure messages, transaction status visibility, retry guidance, and resolution flows.
Defined 6 product KPIs:
Transaction success rate
Failure rate
Retry rate
Recovery rate
Resolution time
Repeat-failure rate
Prioritized improvements using user impact, failure frequency, resolution effort, and expected business impact.
Proposed contextual recovery flows to reduce confusion and unnecessary repeated payment attempts.
📅 Aditya AI — Appointment Scheduling Product

Product Area: Consumer Product • AI • Scheduling

A customer-facing appointment scheduling product designed around the problem of customers spending time contacting businesses to check availability and manage appointments.

Product Work
Designed workflows for 4 core actions: booking, cancellation, rescheduling, and availability checking.
Created a multi-business experience covering 50 demo businesses.
Mapped the customer journey across 6 stages from business selection to appointment confirmation.
Defined product metrics including booking completion rate, cancellation rate, rescheduling rate, availability-to-booking conversion, and average steps per booking.
Prioritized high-frequency customer actions to simplify the initial product experience.
Designed voice and text interaction flows around common customer intents.
